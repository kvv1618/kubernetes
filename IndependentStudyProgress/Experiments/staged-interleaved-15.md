# Experiment: Staged Interleaved Rollout with 15sec delay
- Scenario: interleaved | Delay: 15s between arrivals
    - Applied files in this order:
        - cpu.yaml
        - mem.yaml
        - heavy-cpu-request.yaml
        - heavy-mem-util.yaml
        - heavy-cpu-util.yaml
        - heavy-mem-request.yaml
## Before deployment:
- All workers nearly ideal.
## During deployments:
- All pods were being scheduled on their respective workers as expected with the finalScore being in Zone 1
- Pod q42hd — first over-prov-mem pod:
    - It was scheduled on worker-3.
    - Worker-2, which was leading till now, had projectedMem=0.89 indicating that the node was in zone 2, the warning zone. Penality factors got introduced and the finalScore is resuced to 0.54.
    - Worker-3, which had 0 pods scheduled till now, which would definetly lag behind by a significant margin on traditional bin-packing score, had produced a score of 0.56, which was higher than worker-2.
    - The memoryPenality factor of 0.92 almost entirely zeroed out worker-2's memory contribution to the final score, collapsing a strong bin-packing node into a weak one in this scenario as expected.
- Pod xf5wb — second over-prov-mem pod (after q42hd placed on worker3)
    - Worker-2 was unchanged. It should still ideally lead score in traditional bin-pack, as worker-3 has only one pod scheduled with relatively low resource utilization.
    - Worker-3 generates a score of 0.61, which is higher than worker-2's score of 0.54, and gets the pod scheduled on it.
- Wihtout penality and tiered scoring, all the memory over-provisioned pods had gone onto worker-2 in the last experiment - `staged-gradual-15.md`. With this penality and score zone logic in the algorithm, two of the pods were placed on worker-3 to avoid resource overcommitment and risky behavior.
- _Note: We see pods getting killed with OOM error after being scheduled onto the nodes. This is because the pods run a stress command, which tries to allocate 900M of memory once started, irrespective of the resource requests and limits_
